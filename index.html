About Us

 A community, not a building: We are a volunteer-run devotional community and do not have a physical premises. Our satsangs and gatherings are hosted in turns at members' homes and venues around Queenstown — follow our Facebook page or check the Events section below for what's coming up.

Our Mission

To preserve our religion and spiritual practices — living them ourselves and passing them on, undiluted, to our coming generations.

Kirtan & Bhajans

Devotional singing together — kirtans, bhajans and aarti that fill our gatherings with bhakti and keep our hearts connected to the Divine.

Satsang & Devotional Gatherings

Regular sittings for prayer, katha and spiritual company — the heart of our community life in Queenstown.

Religious Celebrations

Observing our sacred festivals in their true devotional form — with puja, aarti, katha and prasad at the centre, not just the occasion.

🕊 Purely Spiritual

Our focus is devotional and religious — Bhakti, Satsang and Seva. Queenstown has wonderful groups for cultural and social celebrations; our place is the spiritual side.

🌟 For Our Coming Generations

Our children should grow up knowing their prayers, scriptures, and practices — not just about them. We keep the flame alive so it passes on naturally.

🤝 Together, Never Apart

Our purpose is never to divide or isolate from anyone — we happily stand alongside all of Queenstown's communities. We simply hold space for our faith to survive and thrive.

Regular Satsang Sittings

Fortnightly & Monthly Sittings — Hosted in Turns

We recommend regular fortnightly or monthly satsang sittings — kirtan, bhajans, katha and prayer together. As we have no premises of our own, we warmly encourage members to come forward and offer their home as a venue. Hosting rotates in turns, so no one family carries the load — and the regularity stays easy to keep.

Hosting a sitting is itself a beautiful seva — if you'd like to offer your space, let us know when you join or simply get in touch.
